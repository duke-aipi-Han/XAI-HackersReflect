# Reflections on Hacker's Mind by Bruce Schneier
## Project for Duke AIPI XAI by Han Wang

Key takeaways from this book:
* Any system of rules or norms can be hacked .
* Loopholes (enabling hacks) often live in the gap between a rule and its purpose.
* Hacking is more than ingenuity, it is also about power (and wealth).
* A hack can become a feature or prompt a rule change when normalized.
* AI could change who finds hacks and how quickly they spread (and how to find hacks before they're exploited).

Salient Examples of Hacks:
* Pudding for Miles (where the metrics for purchases per mile gives very different results for $ per mile)
* gerrymandering (where simple grid of the votes and how it is split gives very different outcomes)
* buying out the lottery (when the payoff is greater than investment needed to buy)
